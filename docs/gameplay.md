# Gameplay

Tiny Factory has a finite 64×64 world with grass, dirt, sand, water, and rock. Gather deposits, build miners and conveyors, feed crafters, and produce ten mechanical parts. The world continues after the objective is reached.

Construction costs: miner 4 stone and 2 iron ore; conveyor 1 stone; crafter 6 stone and 4 iron ore. A miner extracts one item per 60 ticks. A conveyor advances one item per five ticks. Recipes are iron plate (2 iron ore, 40 ticks), copper plate (2 copper ore, 40 ticks), and mechanical part (2 iron plates + 1 wood, 60 ticks).
