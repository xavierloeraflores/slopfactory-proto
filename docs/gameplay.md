# Gameplay

Tiny Factory has a finite 64×64 world with grass, dirt, sand, water, and rock. Gather deposits, build miners and conveyors, feed crafters, and produce ten mechanical parts. The world continues after the objective is reached.

Construction costs: miner 4 stone and 2 iron ore; conveyor 1 stone; crafter 6 stone and 4 iron ore. A miner extracts one item per 60 ticks. A conveyor advances one item per five ticks. Recipes are iron plate (2 iron ore, 40 ticks), copper plate (2 copper ore, 40 ticks), and mechanical part (2 iron plates + 1 wood, 60 ticks).

Start with 20 stone, 12 iron ore, and 5 wood. Build in range of the player. Miners sit on ore or stone deposits and output in their facing direction to an adjacent belt. The crafter accepts matching ingredients from neighboring belts and exports products in its facing direction. Outputs stay buffered when downstream belts are full. Trees supply wood by hand.

Right click a structure to inspect it. For a crafter, E opens recipe and inventory controls: F1 selects iron plate, F2 copper plate, and F3 mechanical part. Tab cycles the item, I transfers from the player into the input, U takes unused input back, and O takes finished output. Hold Shift for a five-item transfer. The HUD shows inventories, production progress, pause state, and speed.
